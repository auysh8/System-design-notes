<div class="note-section">
    <p>Relational databases ensure strict transactional safety through <mark class="hl-pink">ACID guarantees</mark>:</p>

    <div class="card-grid">
        <StickyNote type="mint" title="🅰️ Atomicity">
            <p style="font-size:0.9rem;">Transactions execute completely or fail entirely ("All or Nothing"). Partial updates are rolled back.</p>
        </StickyNote>

        <StickyNote type="sky" title="🅲 Consistency">
            <p style="font-size:0.9rem;">Moves data from one valid state to another valid state, maintaining schema constraints.</p>
        </StickyNote>

        <StickyNote type="lavender" title="🅸 Isolation">
            <p style="font-size:0.9rem;">Concurrent transactions execute without dirty reads or interference from parallel tasks.</p>
        </StickyNote>

        <StickyNote type="yellow" title="🅳 Durability">
            <p style="font-size:0.9rem;">Committed data changes remain permanently stored, even during power or hardware failure.</p>
        </StickyNote>
    </div>
</div>
