        Transport Layer Overview: Reliability vs. Speed
    </div>

    <p>The <mark class="hl-blue">Transport Layer (Layer 4)</mark> serves as the foundational communication bridge between application processes running on different host machines across IP networks. It manages end-to-end data transport, session establishment, packet segmentation, and error recovery.</p>

    {/* HERO COMPARISON CARDS */}

            </p>
            <div style="margin-top: 0.85rem; padding: 0.6rem 0.85rem; background: var(--paper-bg); border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-soft); font-size: 0.82rem; color: var(--text-muted);">

                <span class="planner-tag" style="background: var(--pastel-orange); color: var(--pastel-orange-dark); margin: 0; font-size: 0.75rem;">Fire-and-Forget</span>
            </div>
            <p style="font-size: 0.92rem; line-height: 1.6;">
                Transmits independent datagram chunks immediately without prior session negotiation. Has no acknowledgment system, no retransmissions, and no packet reordering logic.
            </p>
            <div style="margin-top: 0.85rem; padding: 0.6rem 0.85rem; background: var(--paper-bg); border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-soft); font-size: 0.82rem; color: var(--text-muted);">

{/* SECTION 2: TCP DEEP DIVE */}
<div class="note-section">
    <div class="section-title">
        <span class="title-badge" style="background: var(--pastel-green);">2</span>
        TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) Deep-Dive
    </div>

    <p><mark class="hl-green">TCP</mark> is engineered for guaranteed reliability. It treats communication as a continuous, reliable byte stream rather than isolated messages.</p>

    <StickyNote type="sky" title="🔒 The 4 Pillars of TCP Reliability:">
        <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(220px, 1fr)); gap: 0.75rem; margin-top: 0.5rem;">
            <div style="background: var(--paper-card); padding: 0.65rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-soft);">
                <strong>1. Delivery Guarantee:</strong> Every packet is tracked. Missing packets are automatically resent.
            </div>
            <div style="background: var(--paper-card); padding: 0.65rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-soft);">
                <strong>2. Ordered Delivery:</strong> Sequence numbers ensure segments are reassembled in exact order.
            </div>
            <div style="background: var(--paper-card); padding: 0.65rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-soft);">
                <strong>3. Flow Control:</strong> Prevents a fast sender from overwhelming a slow receiver buffer (Sliding Window).
            </div>
            <div style="background: var(--paper-card); padding: 0.65rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-soft);">
                <strong>4. Congestion Control:</strong> Adjusts sending rates based on network capacity (Slow Start, AIMD).
            </div>
        </div>
    </StickyNote>

    <div class="card-grid" style="margin-top: 1.5rem;">
        <GoodNotesCard title="🌊 Flow Control: Sliding Window" accent="blue">
            <p style="font-size: 0.9rem;">
                The receiver continuously advertises its available buffer space (<strong>Receive Window: rwnd</strong>) in every ACK packet. The sender ensures in-flight unacknowledged bytes never exceed this limit, preventing buffer overflow and dropped frames.
            </p>
        </GoodNotesCard>

        <GoodNotesCard title="🚦 Congestion Control: AIMD" accent="green">
            <p style="font-size: 0.9rem;">
                <strong>Additive Increase / Multiplicative Decrease:</strong> The sender starts with a small Congestion Window (cwnd) during <em>Slow Start</em>, scales linearly while ACKs arrive smoothly, and aggressively cuts the rate in half upon detecting packet loss.
            </p>
        </GoodNotesCard>
    </div>

    <div style="margin-top: 1.25rem;">
        <GoodNotesCard title="💼 Prime Production Use Cases for TCP:" accent="green">
            <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(230px, 1fr)); gap: 0.75rem; margin-top: 0.5rem; font-size: 0.88rem;">
                <div style="background: var(--paper-bg); padding: 0.6rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 3px solid var(--pastel-green-dark);">
                    <strong>💳 Payments & Banking:</strong> Stripe, PayPal, SWIFT transfers (zero tolerance for dropped transactions).
                </div>
                <div style="background: var(--paper-bg); padding: 0.6rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 3px solid var(--pastel-blue-dark);">
                    <strong>🌐 Web & APIs:</strong> HTTPS / REST / GraphQL / gRPC / WebSocket over TLS.
                </div>
                <div style="background: var(--paper-bg); padding: 0.6rem 0.85rem; border-left: 3px solid var(--pastel-purple-dark); border-radius: 8px;">
                    <strong>🔑 Auth & Tokens:</strong> OAuth2, JWT verification, session cookie updates.

                    <strong>📁 File & Data Storage:</strong> SFTP, FTP, database replication streams (PostgreSQL/MySQL WAL logs).

            </div>
        </GoodNotesCard>

{/* SECTION 3: UDP DEEP DIVE */}
<div class="note-section">
    <div class="section-title">
        <span class="title-badge" style="background: var(--pastel-yellow);">3</span>
        UDP (User Datagram Protocol) Deep-Dive
    </div>

    <p><mark class="hl-yellow">UDP</mark> strips away connection state, flow control, and retransmission logic in exchange for the lowest possible latency and minimal CPU/network overhead.</p>

    <StickyNote type="yellow" title="⚡ The 4 Pillars of UDP Architecture:">
        <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(220px, 1fr)); gap: 0.75rem; margin-top: 0.5rem;">
            <div style="background: var(--paper-card); padding: 0.65rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-soft);">
                <strong>1. 0-RTT Connectionless:</strong> No handshake setup or teardown. First packet carries actual application data.
            </div>
            <div style="background: var(--paper-card); padding: 0.65rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-soft);">
                <strong>2. Head-of-Line Free:</strong> Dropping packet #3 does not stall packet #4 from reaching the application immediately.
            </div>
            <div style="background: var(--paper-card); padding: 0.65rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-soft);">
                <strong>3. Ultralight 8B Overhead:</strong> Maximizes useful data payload ratio per Ethernet frame (MTU 1500 bytes).
            </div>
            <div style="background: var(--paper-card); padding: 0.65rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-soft);">
                <strong>4. Multicast / Broadcast:</strong> Able to stream one packet to thousands of receivers simultaneously.
            </div>
        </div>
    </StickyNote>

    <div class="card-grid" style="margin-top: 1.5rem;">
        <GoodNotesCard title="🚫 Eliminating Head-of-Line (HoL) Blocking" accent="pink">
            <p style="font-size: 0.9rem;">
                In TCP, if segment 3 is lost, segments 4 and 5 must sit buffered in the operating system kernel until segment 3 is retransmitted. <strong>UDP passes segments directly to the application layer</strong> as soon as they arrive, preserving real-time flow.
            </p>
        </GoodNotesCard>

        <GoodNotesCard title="📡 Broadcast & IP Multicast" accent="yellow">
            <p style="font-size: 0.9rem;">
                Because UDP doesn't maintain two-way connection state machines, a single sender can broadcast datagrams across an entire LAN subnet or multicast stock market quotes to millions of subscribing trading terminals.
            </p>
        </GoodNotesCard>
    </div>

    <div style="margin-top: 1.25rem;">
        <GoodNotesCard title="🎮 Prime Production Use Cases for UDP:" accent="purple">
            <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(230px, 1fr)); gap: 0.75rem; margin-top: 0.5rem; font-size: 0.88rem;">
                <div style="background: var(--paper-bg); padding: 0.6rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 3px solid var(--pastel-pink-dark);">
                    <strong>🎮 Multiplayer Gaming:</strong> Player positions & physics state (a stale coordinate resent 200ms late is useless).
                </div>
                <div style="background: var(--paper-bg); padding: 0.6rem 0.85rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 3px solid var(--pastel-purple-dark);">
                    <strong>📹 Real-Time Audio/Video:</strong> WebRTC, Zoom, Discord, Google Meet (prefer brief glitch over audio lag).
                </div>
                <div style="background: var(--paper-bg); padding: 0.6rem 0.85rem; border-left: 3px solid var(--pastel-yellow-dark); border-radius: 8px;">
                    <strong>🔍 DNS Lookups (Port 53):</strong> Lightweight single-request domain-to-IP resolution queries.
                </div>
                <div style="background: var(--paper-bg); padding: 0.6rem 0.85rem; border-left: 3px solid var(--pastel-blue-dark); border-radius: 8px;">
                    <strong>📊 Metrics & Telemetry:</strong> StatsD, Prometheus UDP exporters, syslog log shippers (high throughput).
                </div>
            </div>
        </GoodNotesCard>
    </div>
</div>
