{/*  SECTION 2: 3 STRATEGIES TO ELIMINATE SPOF  */}
<div class="note-section">
    <div class="section-title">
        <span class="title-badge" style="background: var(--pastel-blue);">2</span>
        3 Strategies to Avoid Load Balancer SPOFs
    </div>

    <div class="card-grid">

        {/*  Strategy 1: Redundancy  */}
        <GoodNotesCard title="🔄 1. Redundancy" accent="blue">
            <p>Adding <mark class="hl-blue">multiple active/standby load balancers</mark> to your infrastructure setup.</p>
            <div style="margin-top: 0.75rem; font-size: 0.85rem; color: var(--text-muted);">
                💡 If one load balancer goes down, secondary load balancers immediately handle and route incoming traffic to backend servers without downtime.
            </div>
        </GoodNotesCard>

        {/*  Strategy 2: Health Checks & Monitoring  */}
        <GoodNotesCard title="🩺 2. Health Checks & Monitoring" accent="green">
            <p>Constantly perform automated <mark class="hl-green">health check probes</mark> on load balancers.</p>
            <div style="margin-top: 0.75rem; font-size: 0.85rem; color: var(--text-muted);">
                💡 Identifies failing load balancers instantly and stops sending traffic to unhealthy nodes until they recover and come back online.
            </div>
        </GoodNotesCard>

        {/*  Strategy 3: Self-Healing Systems  */}
        <GoodNotesCard title="🛠️ 3. Self-Healing Systems" accent="purple">
            <p>Automatically spin up a <mark class="hl-purple">new instance replacement</mark> if a load balancer crashes.</p>
            <div style="margin-top: 0.75rem; font-size: 0.85rem; color: var(--text-muted);">
                💡 Automated orchestration (e.g. Auto-Scaling Groups) detects failure and provisions a fresh load balancer node in place of the dead instance automatically.
            </div>
        </GoodNotesCard>

    </div>
</div>
