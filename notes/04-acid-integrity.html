Transactional Integrity & ACID Properties

Ensuring Reliable Database Transactions

Relational databases ensure strict transactional safety through ACID guarantees:

🅰️ Atomicity

Transactions execute completely or fail entirely ("All or Nothing"). Partial updates are rolled back.

🅲 Consistency

Moves data from one valid state to another valid state, maintaining schema constraints.

🅸 Isolation

Concurrent transactions execute without dirty reads or interference from parallel tasks.

🅳 Durability

Committed data changes remain permanently stored, even during power or hardware failure.
